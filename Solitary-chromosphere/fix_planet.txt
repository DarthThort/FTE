FIX for "Location: Unknown" bug:

In GameState.js constructor (around line 140), REMOVE this line:
        this.initializeGalaxy();

The galaxy initialization should ONLY happen in loadGame(), not in the constructor.

This is causing the galaxy to initialize twice:
1. First in constructor line 140 - sets currentPlanet to Earth
2. Again in loadGame() line 238 if no save exists - OVERWRITES everything

By removing line 140, the galaxy will only initialize once in loadGame().
